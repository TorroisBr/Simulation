## P10 — procedural local topology

**Player/world direction.** A world may contain many Ruin instances. Their generated places and connections can vary by world and site while remaining reproducible from the same compatible genesis inputs, stable site identity, seed/random context and rules. Similar or identical results may occur by chance. Richer structural rules and system-specific content can be added later without making the core topology a dungeon loot/encounter format.

**Delivered audit.** P10-A composes one authored Ruin at the selected P9-B Location with a fixed finite topology and no local randomness. Its `Entrance → Courtyard → Inner Chamber` fixture and `P10RuinLocalTopologyGenesis` class name are bounded implementation facts, not a universal layout or generator contract. The current profile uses `ExplorableSiteData.DefinitionId` as the semantic site key because it has one instance; a world with multiple instances of the same archetype needs a distinct stable site-instance identity. P9-B currently supplies one authored Location. Preserve the P10-A profile and its historical State; do not relabel its authored layout as procedural generation.

**Recommended next checkpoint: P10-B — Deterministic Local Topology Generation v1.** In a new explicit pre-start profile, generate a small finite semantic place/connection graph for one Ruin instance at an already canonical Location. The proof must make local structural choices from a versioned, compatible ruleset and a site-scoped deterministic random context derived from world/genesis inputs plus stable site-instance identity; it cannot select one of a tiny list of whole-layout templates. The bounded design must introduce the minimal instance-versus-definition identity seam, preserve P10-A compatibility and P8/P9 owner/anchor validation, and publish one complete topology atomically before the first simulated boundary. Test same-input reproduction, changed compatible seed/site inputs, deterministic order/IDs, valid entry/connectivity/containment and failure without partial publication. Chosen fixtures should show that the algorithm can produce more than one graph, without requiring every pair of sites to differ.

P10-B consumes promoted P9 ordered genesis/provenance, P8 Location/site anchor, and P10-A LocalTopology ownership. Its technical design must settle the site-instance migration, random-stream derivation, rule revision and finite graph limits before implementation. A second published Ruin in one world additionally needs a profile with a second canonical Location; P10-B need not mint geography to prove per-site generation. Later checkpoints may add multiple sites and separate local content/ruleset layers. P10-B excludes room contents, loot, inhabitants, traps, encounters, OSR tables, other RPG-system schemas, runtime expansion/retrofit and a universal dungeon engine. **Classification:** `READY_FOR_TECHNICAL_DESIGN`, not implementation.

## P14 — one material model, incremental proofs

**Player/world direction.** Distinct sources create or expose material; source-specific reserve/availability constrains output when applicable. Produced stock has a custodian and possibly a different title owner. Local demand can consume it; a market may express opportunity; an individual or organization may decide to retain, sell or move it; transport changes place/custody through a separate causal operation. City is a place and may be a title holder in one profile, but is not the universal economic actor. Finite depletion, multiple sources and trade are compatible pieces of this model, not competing futures.

**Delivered audit.** P14-A proves one identified exogenous daily source for one item in one City, with City/settlement title, aggregate market custody and local free consumption. It has no physical reserve, inputs, transport or autonomous trade decision. Its exogenous quantity is explicit for that profile; it does not mean every `ProductiveSource` is infinite or that all material belongs to a City. `LocalMerchantReserveRatio` is a merchant money policy, not a source's physical reserve. P14-A remains valid and is not retroactively changed.

## P20 — shared activity, different participation patterns

**Player/world direction.** A heavy-stone task may require four workers before start. A harvest may start with one and accept optional helpers whose output effect need not be linear. A factory may require one supervisor and at least one operator, allow several operators and optional loaders. Required headcount/roles answer **whether start is valid**; the domain's contribution rules answer **what each participant changes** in duration, throughput or outcome. One role may hold several people; roles may have different counts and intervals. Participants remain individual actors; shared Activity is not persistent Group membership.

**Delivered audit.** P18's lifecycle accepts a participant collection and owns temporal commitments. P20-A's synthetic owner explicitly requires exactly two Persons, but its State labels that as a fixture detail and its synthetic results remain participant-specific. No generic role/requirement or contribution schema is delivered. The canonical architecture already permits min/max and roles but did not state validity versus contribution as explicitly as this direction requires. No closed P18 scope or promoted P20-A behavior is reopened.

**Recommended next checkpoint: P20-B — Two-Person Joint Civil Travel v1.** Two Persons independently agree to one common civil leg, reserve compatible time, and start only when both are available and the current passage/position facts validate. One shared activity instance coordinates that start; each Person retains identity, Knowledge, position and individual outcome. Reject stale or partially valid starts coherently; define cancellation/abort and release of commitments for this slice. The selected proving requirement may be two equivalent traveler slots, but the capability contract must retain role/count requirements separately from domain contribution and must not encode exactly two, travel-only, identical effect, one permanent party or one universal interval as Activity invariants.

P20-B consumes promoted P18-A/B/C, P20-A's bounded coordination seams, and the applicable P8-E one-Person explicit travel/position/passage capability. The technical design must close the integration from one shared start to two coherent Person-owned travel transitions; P8-E does not already implement joint travel or automatic P18 progression. P11 is conditional if external Actor/GM commands are selected, not required for the controlled proof. P14 active production, P15 construction, P19 loader, persistent Group and War remain outside this checkpoint. **Classification:** `READY_FOR_TECHNICAL_DESIGN`, not implementation.

## Cross-phase coherence and gates

The future chain `finite source → workers/organization → stock → local demand → market opportunity → knowledgeable merchant/guild decision → coordinated transport → destination stock → depletion/shortage` is a coherence test, not one checkpoint. P10 may generate a Mine/site topology without owning its content or production. P14 owns source/reserve/material facts and transactions. P20 coordinates people without owning production, stock or travel truth. P8 owns spatial facts; P18 owns time; P12/P13 later continue/reconstruct admitted authoritative state. No Phase is reopened by this direction alone.

P10-B, P14-B and P20-B need bounded technical designs and independent reviews before implementation dispatch. New owners or new fields in admitted owners need §92A exact-state/reconstruction seams and the P12 `UnityBootstrap-Daily-v1` exclusion/rejection proof before domain promotion; no checkpoint silently expands that profile. The broader future model does not authorize procedural content, autonomous trade AI, generic role solver, framework or loader implementation now.
