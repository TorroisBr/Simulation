## Semantic and restore contract

All six sections stay Required, including at zero rows. The three Office sections share one OfficeStore; both claim sections share one PoliticalClaimStore. InstitutionStore increments once per successful registration. OfficeStore increments on successful office registration, assignment, vacancy and private historical insertion. PoliticalClaimStore increments on claim/recognition/resolution writes. Recognition update preserves History and can advance revision while cardinality stays one. Failed, stale, invalid, faulted or overflow writes preserve state/order/revision. Keep typed IDs, ordinal sorting, mutation-history ordering, target kinds/values, evidence, failure/null/exception behavior and atomicity.

**Office restored clone defect is source-proven:** RegisterOffice produces revision 1; TryAssignIncumbent produces revision 2 and appends an open tenure; TryVacateOffice produces revision 3 and replaces that row in place with its closed value. Current `SimulationRuntime.CloneOfficeStore` registers the office and, with no active incumbency in source, only re-adds the closed historical tenure: the clone is revision 2. It also assigns active rows before appending closed history, which can reorder `TenureHistoryInMutationOrder`. `TryGetLatestClosedTenure` scans that order backwards and compares OfficeId by value, so order is observable. P12 capture already reads `TenureHistoryInMutationOrder`, not sorted `TenureHistory`; schema/codec ordering can stay unchanged.

Keep ordinary Runtime Office clone/replay semantically and behaviorally unchanged. Its only assembly adaptation is that the existing closed-history `TryAddHistoricalTenure` call routes through the narrow `TryAddHistoricalTenureForRuntimeOfficeClone` official bridge; all caller validation, order and failure translation stay in Runtime. For restored continuation with non-null source, retain existing Runtime `CloneOfficeStore` replay as its unchanged validation pass: office registration, active-incumbency PersonStore membership, closed-row structural admission, and original failure/ArgumentException translation. This Runtime helper does not validate historical-tenure Person membership or currentDay bounds; keep the separate existing P12E detached snapshot validation of tenure-person relations and nonnegative/ordered dates, plus the existing P12-G graph checks, in their current stages. No new general time/person policy is added. Discard its replayed candidate and call `InstitutionOfficeOwnerBridge.TryCloneForRestoredContinuation(source, resolvedInstitutionStore, out clone)`. The bridge places the immutable OfficeRecord, OfficeIncumbency, and OfficeTenureRecord references/values into fresh owner collections in exact source collection order, then calls the existing internal exact-owner factory with exact target InstitutionStore and `source.Revision`; it does not allocate new row objects. Return the unpublished candidate; do not replay active-first or append closed rows. If exact factory unexpectedly rejects the already-validated source, Runtime maps it to the existing invalid-office ArgumentException category. Null source keeps current empty revision-zero path and skips bridge. Bind/validate candidate in existing restore order. This preserves supported revision 3 and source mutation order without changing normal construction.

**PoliticalClaim restored clone defect is source-proven:** claim registration revision 1, recognition registration revision 2, update of that same recognition revision 3 while cardinality remains one; replay of claim plus one recognition yields 2. Existing `PoliticalClaimStore.Clone()` copies immutable rows and exact revision; P12 claim stage also uses saved revision. Always execute the existing `ClonePoliticalClaimStore(source, personStore, institutionStore, officeStore, propertyOwnershipStore, currentDay)` replay first, including for restored contexts: that replay is the current validation pass for claimant membership, target existence by kind, created/resolution days, recognizing institution membership, recognition day bounds, cardinality and existing ArgumentException mapping. Route its internal recognition registration through `TryRegisterRecognitionForRuntimeClone`. For a restored context with non-null source, discard only the successfully validated replay candidate and then call `PoliticalClaimOwnerBridge.CloneForRestoredContinuation(source)` to retain exact revision. For ordinary construction or null source, retain the replay result. Never branch directly to the exact clone before replay validation. Preserve recognition history/order and exact restored revision. Do not mint simulated advances/tokens during restore.

Existing Runtime operation scopes and commit callbacks remain the only P12 invalidation path. Preserve one exact InstitutionStore/OfficeStore identity edge. On restored failure keep the previous world and active slot; validate roots/values, bind original guard, recapture owner vector and publish once.
