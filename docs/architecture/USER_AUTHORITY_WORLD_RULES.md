# User authority, world rules and explicit interventions

**Decision:** Product direction approved by the user. This architecture candidate consolidates it in [Simulation architecture §81](../SIMULATION_ARCHITECTURE.md). The decision applies to Simulation, Dungeon Studio and future City Studio and world authoring tools. Its authority comes from explicit authoring or an admitted Core intervention. The specific capabilities below still have separate design and implementation gates.

## Three distinct paths

| Path | What it may do | When it becomes World Truth |
|---|---|---|
| Procedural generation | Apply the versioned effective generation rules and satisfy the selected output contract. Failure produces diagnostics. | A compatible Core genesis/runtime authority admits the exact output and publishes its owners at the correct boundary. |
| Natural simulation | Evaluate normal domain policy, actor ability, Knowledge, material flow and temporal/traversal rules under admitted inputs. A player steering an actor still selects a normal action. | Its usual Core owner commits the supported effect. |
| Explicit user authorship and intervention | Author and accept an exact creative statement in a document, or submit an authorized external declaration with a typed effect. A named capability may permit explicitly enumerated exceptions to normal policy. | Pre-start authorship enters complete initial World Truth through supported genesis. A post-start effect enters through the applicable Core transaction and at its actual logical boundary. |

A suggestion, preview, unsaved edit, document acceptance, submitted request, committed result and presentation annotation convey different claims. Studio can own its documents. Core alone owns authoritative simulation state. Lab can submit supported intent through the official application boundary when authorized. World Atlas remains a read-only consumer. No client derives a new fact from a drawing, a caption, a stock delta or a successful preview.

An exceptional fact persists as the actual fact. Record its external cause as such. Subsequent natural operations evaluate it under their current admitted rules. They may be unable to use it or produce a later supported outcome, but must not silently repair it or retroactively invent a natural cause. User permission for one effect confers no blanket future override, no automatic actor Knowledge and no implied institutional or downstream consequence.

## Classification by effect and consumer

| Constraint | Ordinary policy that a named effect can bypass | Integrity that remains required |
|---|---|---|
| Geometry and topology | Overlap/touch of otherwise representable regions, physical plausibility, routing feasibility and generation aesthetic rules. | Valid bounded geometry for its stored representation, IDs, endpoint references, floor/space types, explicit edges and containment integrity. Consumer-required physical validation still applies when the consumer claims geometry-derived behavior. |
| Placement and traversal | A typed declared placement can ignore route availability, distance, normal passage, cost and travel duration. | Destination exists as a supported typed reference. Exact presence/position, ownership of state, and affected reservations, travel, active party or expedition references remain coherent. Reject where the supported command cannot reconcile them atomically. A direct placement does not traverse a passage or imply an arrival event. |
| Resources and economy | A declaration can create stock without a natural source, exchange or production precondition, or declare an allowed outcome outside normal decision rules. | Item definition, target owner and context exist. Quantities and numeric bounds, item custody/uniqueness, inventory representation and each participating owner's revision and relations remain valid. Supply/currency conservation applies to a transfer or sale. A grant adds an explicitly attributable source and is a different operation. |
| Commands and history | Subject to trusted authority, a supported declaration may bypass ordinary decision causality and ForceOutcome may provide a supported explicit resolution. | Stable world and entity identity, authenticated/admitted principal and capability, validation of target/type/preconditions, ordering, all-or-nothing publication, honest provenance and recoverability. A profile must actually support continuation of every resulting state and any future-affecting idempotency or pending input it admits. |

A validator's name or diagnostic severity does not classify a rule. For any new override, identify at the operation boundary the exact policy deliberately replaced, state that remains invariant, and readers and simulation consumers affected. A duplicate axial coordinate in the current P8 authority would break an identity/index invariant, even though a two-layer fictional map could represent two locations at the same drawing position. Use the appropriate existing identity/layout model or establish a new representation explicitly. A self-intersecting ring cannot be accepted as a simple polygon. Distinct simple polygons that overlap can remain structurally sound.

## Core-owned intervention contract

Reuse the **WorldCommand semantics** of §§81–85. `Suggest` previews, `Request` evaluates the natural operation, `Declare` declares a typed fact, and `ForceOutcome` supplies an explicit result only in a matching reviewed resolver. Existing DTO flags are not a privilege grant. The application derives authority/origin and responsible identity from the trusted host context and target world. Reject self-attested GM flags, wrong/foreign WorldId, invalid IDs or links, out-of-profile effects, stale expected cut/versions, unsupported operation/override, and unsupported durable state. Preview and translation stay pure. Revalidate at application and effect admission, including an omitted or stale preview. The host controls the ingress policy; the domain owns its effect and invariants.

The minimum future slice is one synchronous typed declaration at a supported healthy boundary, with no queue, cross-world transaction, or free-form state patch. Its technical design must bind these inputs and outputs:

1. A command kind/version and idempotency key scoped to the world; the typed canonical payload, target IDs, effective definitions and compatibility identity; authorizing principal/authority and allowed override reason/class when relevant. Optional narrative may describe the intervention separately. It cannot make an effect authoritative.
2. An exact runtime/profile/session binding with the logical application boundary and deterministic Core order. Use the approved daily boundary/order or a later supported P18 instant and same-instant order. Wall clock is presentation/audit metadata. Expected revision/cut and idempotency rules admit a stable retry only for the same normalized intent, effect version and authority scope. A conflicting reuse rejects without another effect. Preserve the required idempotency scope across compatible restore.
3. A private validated plan for the complete write set, including related owners, references, changed revisions, IDs, effect records and retained accepted input. Reserve causal order and validate saturation/capacity before writes. Commit the declared result and the information needed to recover/replay it together under one Core operation. Failure leaves the previous visible World Truth, business revisions, authoritative ordering and accepted input history unchanged. An integrity rollback failure faults admission. Rejected attempts may be audited separately if they cannot affect authoritative identities, ordering, RNG or future execution.
4. A committed intervention disposition with accepted input identity, actual boundary/order and affected facts or an exact effect/result reference. Publish factual changes through approved readers at coherent cuts. A later actual domain event or additional consequence follows its own ordinary operation and provenance. The first slice may apply only the declared effect and required structural bookkeeping. No inferred supplier, harvest, sale, travel, ownership or extra history entry.

For example, a reviewed declaration can grant wood to a contained inventory at a registered ocean location. If there is an applicable installed inventory, an admitted wood definition and representable quantity/cost semantics, Core updates that inventory and records an external grant. No nearby tree, transfer from another party, cash change or completed extraction is inferred. If current composition cannot represent the inventory/position or the operation lacks a coherent grant contract, report the specific missing capability. A place stock declaration and an NPC inventory grant name different owners. An extraordinary event with no existing bounded semantic effect remains a proposal until a domain contract exists.

## Provenance, continuation and future capability gates

Maintain original authored inputs and effective rules or immutable compatible content identity, source doc/version/digest plus retained bytes where needed, accepted request and actual result/provenance. Distinguish complete initial facts established by admitted genesis from a post-start committed input. An authored backstory is not a series of simulated boundaries. Adoption into a live world is a new runtime input, never retroactive regeneration.

For a durable world, retain the full normalized accepted cause, logical application order, versions and authority from the first supported input. Reproduce the effect from compatible executable inputs, or retain the exact authoritative result when replaying that input alone cannot recover it. The chosen checkpoint design must specify what persistence requires of effect state, pending inputs and retry outcomes, their semantic capture/hydration, owner census and epoch where actually applicable, target validation and failure atomicity. Unadmitted effects must fail before mutation in a supported persistent world. `UnityBootstrap-Daily-v1` retains its existing exclusions and formal continuation scope. A future supported profile/capability and its validation must be explicit; storing a fact in an already-known inventory owner by itself does not admit a new causal operation.

P12 continuation preserves WorldId at a compatible saved boundary. P13 remains separately gated on recoverable actual history, including accepted interventions before or after the fork point. An eventual fork has a new WorldId and source/boundary provenance. No independent P13 runtime, universal event log or save redesign is needed for the architecture decision. Atlas displays only approved typed provenance. An observed change without a reader for the committed intervention stays an observed difference; no new LAB-OBS-A wire requirement follows.

Rule and intervention admission belong to their own contracts. Future P19 extensions can supply versioned rules or admitted typed capabilities through the approved design path. Their deterministic code/content and effective overrides have to be retained or recoverable. A live rule change is itself an explicit accepted/versioned input with a boundary and compatibility proof. Mod contents or a request mode enum do not confer GM privilege. General loader and arbitrary executable mutation remain deferred.
