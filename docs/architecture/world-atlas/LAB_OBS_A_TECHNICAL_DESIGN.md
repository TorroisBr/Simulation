`AdvanceAndObserve` dispositions are `RejectedBeforeAdvance` (no attempt, no cut), `AdvanceUnsuccessful` (a failed or throwing advance, no cut and no rollback claim), `AdvancedObservationUnavailable` (Core completed a day but read, verification or disposal failed, no cut), and `Captured` (the successful boundary and complete cut). If start, advance, capture or write fails, no new final artifact is published for the failed record and no predecessor link is advanced. See the wire contract for the atomic commit boundary.
