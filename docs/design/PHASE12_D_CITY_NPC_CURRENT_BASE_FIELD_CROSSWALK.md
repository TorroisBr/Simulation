## Decision and scope boundary

The current P12-D contract assigns every retained `NpcRuntime` fact to one D/F projection, with E owning only separate E authorities. E-provider writes to an NPC remain in the assigned D/F field; E must not make another NPC projection. D and F capture the concrete NPC once under the same completed-boundary token and exact revision vector, merge disjoint values, and stage one NPC. This crosswalk adds source-level field, identity, revision, and writer evidence to that contract. It does not add a synthetic `NpcRuntime` revision.

The P12-D shared assembly remains a serialized hotspot. Daily-v1 requires the installed `ExplorableSiteStore` witness `p12d.explorable-sites` to be exact `ExplicitlyEmpty`; no site row or site identity is emitted. P12-E's LocalTopology owner remains typed `NOT_COMPOSED`. P10 Ruin/LocalTopology stays a separate profile. None of this makes City/NPC implementation ready.

## Non-serialized fields and exact capture rule

`runtimeMutationGuardBinding`, `personIdentity`, `personRuntime`, `currentStatusView`, mutation admission/commit delegates, and the local revision counters themselves are not independent serialized facts. They are runtime bindings, caches, views, or witnesses. `travelStateRevision`, `currentActionRevision`, `lifeStateRevision`, `residenceRevision`, and `p12CrimeJusticeRevision` are included only as exact local revision evidence in the transient P12-B vector and where an accepted owner export preserves that owner's revision; they are not a synthetic aggregate revision. Stage must bind them only after exact private owner construction and validation.

Capture must use `Existing*` accessors for potentially lazy Knowledge/plan owners. Calling lazy public getters can instantiate an absent owner and destroy absent-versus-empty evidence. Before/after capture requires the same successful P12-B completed-boundary token and exact owner identity/schema/cardinality/revision vector. Any unclassified mutable field, unsupported writer, missing witness, saturated revision, alias, or mutation during capture rejects the unpublished package.
