## Findings

The semantic boundary is otherwise sound: it covers only the normal selected-profile `MerchantSystem.AdvanceNpcTradeState` call, identifies the exact roster actor and `AbsoluteDay` without claiming a durable receipt or exactly-once behavior, and correctly treats the nested operation count as accounting rather than a lock or quiescence proof. It preserves Knowledge versus World Truth, existing sequential partial-commit semantics, and explicit limits on capture/export/hydration and P12 readiness. P12's accepted partial-scope rules permit uncovered causal roots; they do not require this one operation to cover every P12 owner.

Two technical revisions are required before implementation:

1. **Exclude global allocator and sequence roots from this operation's claim.** The proposed redirect can allocate a decision ID and record sequence. In the normal daily roster loop, an actor's decision recording can occur after one Merchant call and before another actor's Merchant call. Requiring `RuntimeIdAllocator` and `SimulationRecordSequence` in each invocation's preflight/delta accounting would therefore make later valid calls see drift and reject/fault. Preserve the current decision-record and redirect behavior, but remove these sections from this Merchant operation's preflight and epoch claim. Explicitly leave decision IDs, record sequence, and separate decision-store append as uncovered causal-root/read-model writer families for their own P12-C/P12-F treatment. Passive witnesses do not imply coverage; make no whole-profile capture or completeness claim. This bounded exclusion is consistent with the current P12 partial-scope contract.

2. **Define same-owner invalidation for writers outside the nested Merchant call.** The design preflights exact `SpatialKnowledge`, `CommercialKnowledge`, embedded `MerchantTradePlanRuntime`, and `NpcTravelPlanRuntime` sections against accepted baselines. Normal runtime work changes those owners outside the named sub-operation. `SimulationRuntime` enters `runtime.advance-day` at lines 3665–3672 and holds that enclosing operation across `AdvanceDayAfterClockAdvance`. Before the roster Merchant calls, `RefreshLocalKnowledgeAndShare` (lines 3783, 5496–5518) discovers locations, observes markets, and shares CommercialKnowledge. Post-travel observations also run at line 3927. Those writes change shared Knowledge revisions before later per-NPC preflight. Other writers of the same embedded plan owners include `MerchantSystem.TryExecuteAction` (Merchant plan at line 626 and Trade travel at line 938), `CrimeSystem` (flee plan at line 932), and `TravelActionProvider` (plan clearing). They are outside the named Merchant sub-operation, including when invoked during the enclosing daily operation. Without commit notifications, valid earlier writes leave the exact section baselines stale and a later Merchant invocation faults closed.

   The best-supported bounded seam is owner-local monotone revisions plus commit notification at existing `SpatialKnowledge`, `CommercialKnowledge`, `MerchantTradePlanRuntime`, and `NpcTravelPlanRuntime` mutators, bound to exact owner object and NPC `RuntimeId`. Notify every sibling section that shares a revision. The normal `SimulationRuntime` callers above already execute inside admitted `runtime.advance-day`; therefore those owner hooks can report their commits through the enclosing operation, while the named Merchant nested scope aggregates its own changed sections into the proposed single post-commit batch. Specify behavior for bound public mutations outside that admitted owner-thread path (fail closed or explicitly unsupported), while preserving standalone unbound domain/test use. Keep P18-D occurrence execution and any other unmapped writer outside this slice.

These are technical contract corrections, not new product or canonical architecture choices. The accepted P12 prerequisite authorization supports corrected design and implementation after independent exact-tip re-review; it does not make this candidate implementation-ready.
