Actor observations in site/spatial Knowledge stores belong to F only where that future profile's inventory admits those owners. ## 1. Purpose and boundary

P12-D supplies immutable factual-root and relation sections for the accepted `UnityBootstrap-Daily-v1` profile. It captures and reconstructs the already composed City, NPC, Person/population, genealogy, and legacy spatial-network truth, and preserves the exact-empty `p12d.explorable-sites` census section. Daily-v1 rejects populated legacy site state and does not export or hydrate site rows. A future profile may explicitly admit the generic multi-site owner. This design does not define a new profile or add gameplay behavior.

The profile's existing P9-B genesis evidence and P8-A Hex/Location/scale facts are P12-C-owned roots. D references their stable identities where required but does not export, recreate, or infer them. P9-B outputs are historical facts: D hydration does not rerun bootstrap/genesis.

P12-D includes the legacy `SpatialNetworkRuntime` locations/routes used by the selected bootstrap. The selected profile also observes the installed `ExplorableSiteStore` through the exact `p12d.explorable-sites` census section, which must remain `ExplicitlyEmpty` and bound to the P12-B owner/token; any populated site row rejects Daily-v1. Site-row export/hydration is deferred to a future profile that explicitly admits that owner. These legacy authorities remain distinct from canonical P8 spatial authorities. P8-B passage; P8-C City/Site anchors and Person positions; P8-D route plans/Knowledge; P8-E travel; P10 Ruin/LocalTopology; P14 source/material-flow state; P18 temporal state; P19 module/loader state; P20 shared activities; P13 reconstruction/fork history; external WorldCommand queues; `PlaceContentStore`; and optional systems absent from the selected composition are excluded sections. For an excluded composed authority, admission requires an exact empty witness or rejects populated state; P10 LocalTopology's separate Daily-v1 `NOT_COMPOSED` state is consumed through P12-E's typed provider-absence witness and does not require or instantiate an empty owner. Legacy route facts never substitute for P8 identity or canonical routes.

No Sleep, Dreams, robbery/gang, ritual, War gameplay, MegaEvento, procedural terrain, settlement, population, or backstory generation is introduced here.

## 2. Owner boundary and section assignment

Exports are immutable value projections produced by the authority that owns each fact. The future adapter may split a concrete owner's values across semantic checkpoint sections, but it must capture that concrete owner once under one valid P12-B transient capture token and bind each disjoint projection to the exact owner/component revision vector observed for that token. Merge the projections only when their capture token and vector match, then construct/hydrate that owner once. It must not create a second source of truth or duplicate a field across sections.

## 3. Immutable export contract

Each included concrete owner must expose a read-only export operation callable only under the P12-B capture token and owner-serialization window. It returns a detached immutable value graph (or immutable records plus private immutable collections), with stable typed identities and no live Unity/runtime object references. The transient capture stamp and exact component revision vector are identical across merged D/E or D/E/F projections. Enumeration order is explicit and stable; it is preserved if a current consumer can use order as a causal/tie-breaking input.

The exported semantic records should contain only owner facts:

* City factual root: City runtime ID, City definition/content identity, legacy location runtime ID, population aggregate/revision and committed population-operation receipt set/revision, ordered `ImportantNpcs` membership/revision, ordered market-item roots (item identity, amount, desired amount, current price), market revision, counterparty identity/liquidity/account identity/balance/revision, and population-economy identity/payment mode/account identity/balance/revision. These are all D-owned in current Daily-v1; its production/consumption/price operations update D roots. The selected profile has no E City projection. P18 continuation receipts and P14 finite-source state are excluded.
* NPC core: the D/F crosswalk above is normative. D exports `NpcRuntimeId`, definition and current-action-definition identities, D factual fields including exact `hiddenDaysRemaining`, inventory/account, and City/location root references. F exports the mutable action/plan/travel payloads and Knowledge values from the same captured token and exact owner/component revision vector. Person binding is represented through the Person-owned reciprocal `PersonId`↔`NpcRuntimeId` relation, not a copied identity field with separate authority.
* Person: `PersonId`, optional birth/death absolute day, optional residence settlement runtime ID, and optional materialized NPC runtime ID. Preserve Person rows whether materialized or dormant.
* Genealogy: exact parent/child `PersonId` edges from the relation store.
* Legacy spatial: location ID records and route records `(routeRuntimeId, originLocationRuntimeId, destinationLocationRuntimeId, travelDays)`; preserve record order and parallel routes. A legacy route's travel days are the runtime's normalized value (at least one), not an independently recomputed duration.
* Site: for Daily-v1, no site-row record is exported; the required `p12d.explorable-sites` section is exactly empty and bound to its installed owner and P12-B token. Only a future profile that explicitly admits populated site state exports site runtime ID, site-instance ID, site definition ID, store order, and exact legacy location runtime ID. The current site owner has no mutable progress field. Actor exploration observations and spatial Knowledge remain F values in the same single D/F NpcRuntime projection where an admitted profile includes those owners.

The export must be detached: subsequent mutation of the source runtime or an array/list used in an export cannot mutate the captured record graph. It must not export derived reverse indexes (`outgoingRoutes`, Person materialization lookup maps, or site-by-location query results) as independent truth. Preserve the ordered `CityRuntime.ImportantNpcs` owner projection because current actor-selection consumers iterate that exact list; validate each membership entry against the reciprocal NPC current-City link rather than rebuilding it from NPC roster order. Rebuild other indexes only where their owning API defines them as derived; otherwise preserve the owner’s authoritative list/order.

## 4. Staged hydration and dependency order

Hydration runs only into an unpublished private composition. No live runtime is partially overwritten. Owner constructors/factories used for hydration are internal, exact-value paths; ordinary gameplay mutation APIs and bootstrap generators are not used to recreate the saved facts.

1. **Admit and acquire roots (B/C).** P12-B validates the profile, completed boundary, compatible build/content/providers, owner-thread/quiescence, mutation health, and exclusion dispositions. P12-C restores exact typed-ID allocator/high-water roots, record-sequence state, deterministic-random continuation roots, the P9-B manifest/provenance, and selected P8-A facts. The P12-D owner package captures/stages the RuntimeIdentityRegistry section from those exact IDs and D-owned runtime entities; neither C nor D allocates replacement IDs.
2. **Restore legacy spatial locations.** Create exact legacy `SpatialLocationRuntime` objects from stable IDs, register each with the staged `RuntimeIdentityRegistry`/`SpatialNetworkRuntime`, and reject any identity collision. These are legacy locations, not P8 `LocationId` facts.
3. **Construct each City once and retain ordered pending NPC IDs.** This reference order carries forward the single-construction City/NPC owner seam in the P12-D City root snapshot design candidate at `837bcdafa736cc15ef7ecba0e6c20bddfaa1ff7f`; it changes only how the already-owned City/NPC links are assembled during staging. Resolve each City definition and legacy location reference. Capture the City root and the merged D/E/F `NpcRuntime` projection once under the same P12-B completed-boundary token, transient capture stamp, and exact owner-component revision vector; reject split or mismatched capture evidence. The detached City values retain the exact ordered `ImportantNpcs` runtime IDs and `ImportantNpcRevision`, while the transient capture envelope carries the token/stamp/vector only until capture validation completes; none is serialized as owner state. For staging, construct one final `CityRuntime` per City from the exact D roots, including population operation receipts and `ImportantNpcRevision`, with its one private `ImportantNpcs` backing list reserved for ordered members. Retain the captured ordered NPC IDs in the unpublished D/E/F graph builder as pending references; do not sort or rebuild them from NPC roster order. E contributes no City fields. If a separately reviewed future profile admits an E-owned nested field, merge its disjoint projection only when its transient capture stamp and P12-B revision vector exactly match D's. Do not construct a City shell and replace it with a second City or hydrator.
4. **Stage each merged NPC once, then resolve City membership and Person.** Restore NPC core dependencies after the Cities and legacy locations exist. Merge D/E/F projections for each `NpcRuntime` only when they share the same completed-boundary capture token, transient capture stamp, and exact component revision vector, with one semantic owner per field. Construct each final NPC exactly once through its exact-value factory, resolving its captured current-City ID to the already staged City and its current-location ID to the staged legacy location. Assign those direct references in the factory; do not call the gameplay starting-City constructor, `CityRuntime.AddImportantNpc`, or `NpcRuntime.SetCurrentPresence` to hydrate them. Restore `hiddenDaysRemaining` exactly; do not call `HideForDays`, whose gameplay duration semantics include the current day. After all NPC instances exist, resolve each City's pending ordered IDs through the staged NPC identity map and fill that City's private backing list exactly once in captured order, without invoking gameplay mutations or changing `ImportantNpcRevision`. Reject duplicate, dangling, or cross-owner IDs and require bidirectional reciprocity: every City member's `CurrentCity` and `CurrentLocation` reference the exact staged City/location, and every NPC that claims a current City appears exactly once in that City's ordered membership. Any failure discards the unpublished staged composition; no partially linked graph is handed off. Only then restore Person rows with exact PersonIds, dates, and Person-level residences, and establish reciprocal materialization bindings after all NPC identities exist. The staged `PersonStore` rebuilds its derived materialization index from validated pairs. Person residence stays attached to the Person even while a Person is materialized; NPC-only actors may retain their existing NPC-level residence semantics if that is the live owner contract.
5. **Restore population aggregate without transitions.** The City owner hydration installs its captured aggregate value, population revision, committed population-operation receipt set, and receipt revision; it does not replay births, deaths, migrations, or materializations. Validate represented-resident diagnostics against the current domain rule without silently “correcting” the aggregate. Any mismatch rejects the stage for diagnosis.
6. **Restore genealogy after Persons.** The required schema-version-1 Genealogy section is present even for an empty edge set; missing and empty are distinct. Its immutable owner snapshot carries copied, deterministically ordered `(ParentId, ChildId)` records and the exact `GenealogyStore.Revision`. The isolated private factory rebuilds the local graph indexes and restores the revision directly without replaying writes. Revision is nonnegative and no smaller than the direct-edge count; equality is not required because successful removals leave revision gaps, and the existing saturated birth-rollback path can remove an edge while revision remains at `long.MaxValue`. The factory rejects unsupported schema, null/malformed records, duplicate/self/cyclic edges, impossible revisions, and publishes no partial store. Once PersonStore is staged, the merged D graph validator rejects either endpoint absent from PersonStore. Do not add PersonStore lookup to GenealogyStore, synthesize Persons, or rewrite relations from NPC state.
7. **Restore routes and preserve the Daily-v1 Site witness.** Create exact route objects after both endpoint locations are registered; register them in captured order so derived outgoing-route lists have the same order. For Daily-v1, validate the exact P12-B `p12d.explorable-sites` owner/token section as `ExplicitlyEmpty`, reject any live or exported site row, and do not invoke a site-row hydrator. A future profile that explicitly admits populated sites may restore each site only after its exact referenced legacy location and definition are available. Rebuild only owner-derived indexes. Never regenerate a route/site, infer an endpoint, or re-anchor a site through P8 geography.
8. **Validate and hand off.** The D stage returns a private validated owner package to P12-G. G composes it with every other included B–F package, runs whole-graph validation and parity/rejection gates, binds the fresh mutation guard, and performs the profile's single publication. D does not publish a runtime or claim atomic whole-profile replacement by itself.

## 5. Identity, relation, cardinality, and revision rules

* Preserve type distinctions: `NpcRuntimeId` identifies a simulated NPC runtime; `PersonId` identifies a Person across dormant/materialized/dead states; legacy spatial runtime IDs identify legacy locations/routes/sites; P8 Hex/Location IDs are separate P12-C identities. Never derive one ID from another or replace it on restore.
* Require nonempty exact IDs and uniqueness within each owning typed identity domain. The restored P12-C identity registry enforces uniqueness only among generated runtime-ID kinds registered with it; it does not merge `PersonId`, `HexId`, `LocationId`, and runtime-ID namespaces. Duplicate IDs in an applicable owner domain, incompatible definition references, or allocator state unable to continue without collision reject the stage.
* A Person may exist with no `NpcRuntime`. A Person's optional materialized NPC link must be reciprocal and one-to-one; an NPC not linked from a Person remains NPC-only. No Person can bind two NPCs and no NPC can materialize two Persons.
* For a materialized Person-backed NPC, residence truth is Person-level. NPC field projections must not duplicate or override that value. The referenced settlement must exist. Birth/death dates preserve exact nullable absolute-day values and valid ordering. Age and maturity are derived.
* Genealogy owner validation preserves exact directed `PersonId` edge values and its local revision. The isolated store checks non-null valid IDs, unique edges, no self-edge, and acyclicity; the merged D graph then checks that both endpoints exist in PersonStore. Do not apply extra domain rules not already required by the existing genealogy owner.
* Preserve each City aggregate, population revision, committed population-operation receipt set, and receipt revision exactly. Population is not recomputed from represented NPCs during hydration; validation uses the existing population semantics and reports mismatch as failure. Materializing/dematerializing NPCs does not adjust aggregate population.
* Every route has a unique stable runtime ID and registered, distinct origin/destination locations. Multiple route IDs may connect the same pair; this is not a duplicate. Preserve exact stored travel days and route iteration order. A route with an absent endpoint rejects.
* For Daily-v1, `p12d.explorable-sites` is required-empty and no site identity or location reference is restored. In a future profile that explicitly admits populated sites, each site has a unique runtime ID, compatible definition ID and existing legacy location reference; multiple sites may share one location, with no universal one-to-one site/location or City/location cardinality.
* For any admitted populated-site profile, a site/location reference is serialized by its authoritative owner and checked in the merged stage. No dangling or silently nulled reference is accepted. NPC/City/location references remain validated for Daily-v1 under the City/NPC rules above.
* Bind every export to the exact P12-B token and transient capture stamp. D/E City sections and D/E/F NPC sections must carry identical token identity and the exact same relevant P12-B owner-section revision vector. A split, repeated, stale, or cross-runtime capture rejects before export acceptance. Preserve nested owner revisions/receipt revisions such as SettlementPopulationRuntime and MarketRuntime values. Do not synthesize an aggregate City/NPC revision. Any mutable component absent from the P12-B witness vector is a blocker until the existing owner census is updated through its reviewed scope.

## 6. Validation and test contract

Implementation tests should establish owner semantics, not just DTO shape:

1. **Detached export and stable repeat:** capture empty and populated owners, mutate the source, and prove the exported values remain unchanged; repeated capture under the same valid P12-B token and unchanged component vector has stable content/order. Attempted D/E City or D/E/F NPC merges with different capture token/revision vector or overlapping field ownership fail.
2. **City roots:** round-trip City identity/content/location, population aggregate/revision and operation receipts, ordered `ImportantNpcs` membership/revision, market item rows and revision, and counterparty/population-account identities, balances, and revisions. Validate reciprocal NPC current-City links and actor-selection order. Assert one CityRuntime reconstruction and no production/consumption/repricing is executed during hydration; reject populated excluded P18 receipts/P14 material-flow state and any non-empty E City slice for Daily-v1.
3. **Person/population semantics:** cover unmaterialized Person, materialized Person-backed NPC, NPC-only actor, known/unknown birth and death dates, Person-level residence, population after materialization, and a City with aggregate population greater than named residents. Verify no age field is serialized and no hydration/materialization changes aggregate, population revision, or committed population-operation receipts/revision.
4. **NPC hidden-day state:** round-trip NPCs with both zero and positive `hiddenDaysRemaining`, and compare the value and subsequent `AdvanceHiddenDay` behavior after identical later daily inputs. Hydration preserves the current counter exactly; it does not replay `HideForDays` or consume a hidden day during restoration. Reject a malformed negative counter before stage acceptance.
5. **Genealogy:** round-trip a required empty section and a populated multi-generation/diamond graph, preserving deterministic direct-edge order and exact `GenealogyStore.Revision`. Cover add/remove/re-add histories where the final edge count repeats but revision differs, and a saturated revision round trip whose later ordinary writes remain rejected without mutation. Prove exported rows are detached and immutable. Reject missing section, unsupported schema, null collection/row/endpoint, duplicate edge, self-edge, cycle, negative or impossible revision, and leave the active graph untouched on failure. In the later merged D-stage test, separately reject endpoints absent from PersonStore. Confirm records remain edge-owned and no Person/NPC row gains parent or child collections.
6. **Legacy spatial graph:** round-trip empty graph, multiple locations, parallel routes with distinct IDs, and different durations for the same endpoints while preserving route order/outgoing lookup. Reject duplicate location/route IDs, cross-kind collision, absent endpoint, self-route, and malformed identity before publication. Do not collapse parallel routes.
7. **Exploration sites/links:** for the accepted Daily-v1 profile, prove the installed `ExplorableSiteStore` is the exact P12-B-bound owner, its `p12d.explorable-sites` section is `ExplicitlyEmpty`, and any populated or mismatched owner rejects before handoff. Do not export or hydrate site rows in Daily-v1. If a future profile explicitly admits populated sites, separately test zero and multiple sites sharing one legacy location, duplicate site IDs, absent locations, incompatible definitions, and exact identity/definition/location preservation. Never synthesize a P8-C anchor, P8 Location, or P10 LocalTopology. Keep exploration observations and spatial Knowledge in the admitted F projection.
8. **Cross-owner consistency:** deliberately mismatch a Person residence settlement, NPC materialization reciprocal link, City location, or D/E/F capture token/component revision vector and prove the private stage rejects without publishing. For Daily-v1, reject a populated/mismatched `p12d.explorable-sites` section and any non-empty E City projection; consume P12-E's typed `NOT_COMPOSED` LocalTopology witness without requiring a composed-empty owner.
